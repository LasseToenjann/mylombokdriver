About

Behind the wheel on Lombok

My Lombok Driver is a small, family-run transport and tour service based in Senggigi on the west coast. The work is simple: pick guests up on time, drive them safely, show them the island the way a local sees it, and charge what was agreed.

Lombok is still quieter than its neighbour across the strait, and that is the point. There are mornings at Tiu Kelep when nobody else is there. There are viewpoints on the Sembalun road where you can hear the wind. Knowing when to be where is most of the job.

Guests come from all over the world, and most of them arrive with a plan that changes by lunchtime. That is fine. The car is yours for the day.
